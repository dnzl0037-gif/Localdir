Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what information LocalDir ("we", "us") handles when you use our website, and the choices you have. Please replace the bracketed placeholders below with your company details before publishing.

Who we are

[Company legal name], [registered address]. Contact: [privacy contact email].

Information we handle

  • Visitors looking for services: you can browse and search without an account. We do not ask you to register or provide personal details to search.
  • Service providers: when you create a provider account we handle your business or contact name, email address and password, plus the service listings you choose to publish (title, category, description, phone, email, address, hours, website).
  • Email verification: to confirm your email address, we send it to an email delivery service (EmailJS) which emails you a one-time code.
  • Assistant conversations: messages you type into the website assistant are sent to a third-party AI provider (Dahl Inference, which runs DeepSeek models) so it can generate a reply. Please do not enter sensitive personal information.

How we use it

  • To show published service listings, including the provider details a provider chooses to publish, to people searching the site.
  • To let providers sign in and manage their own listings.
  • To answer questions through the assistant.

What is public

Only listings a provider has published are visible to visitors. A published listing shows its description and the contact details the provider entered. Providers can unpublish or delete a listing at any time. Provider passwords are never shown to visitors and are stored as a salted hash, not in plain text.

Storage and security

Account and listing data is stored using your browser's local storage on the device where it was created. Clearing your browser data removes it. We use industry-standard hashing for passwords, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Assistant messages are processed by our AI provider as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices

  • Providers can edit, hide or delete any listing from the provider portal.
  • Providers can delete their account and all their listings from the provider portal.
  • You may contact us to ask about, correct or remove information we hold about you.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser local storage only to keep provider accounts, listings and sign-in sessions working.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
